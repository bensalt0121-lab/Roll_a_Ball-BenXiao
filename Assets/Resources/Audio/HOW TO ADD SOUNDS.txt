HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN MUSIC AND SOUNDS
====================================
The game picks sounds from these folders by itself. To add more, just drag
.mp3 / .ogg / .wav files into the right folder in Unity. No other setup.

Music/City         calm music while walking around the city (plays one after another)
Music/BlackMarket  music in the hidden black market under the sewer
Music/Wanted       chase music when you have 2 or more wanted stars
Cars/Engine        engine loop for traffic cars (loops)
Cars/Horn          honks when a car is stuck behind something
Cars/Siren         police car siren (loops)
City/Ambience      street noise in the city (loops)
Weapons/Gunshot    pistol shot (one short shot per file works best)

If you download from a site that needs a login (freesound, pixabay, etc.),
check the license and write the credit in AUDIO_CREDITS.txt.
